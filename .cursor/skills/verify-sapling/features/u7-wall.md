# Wall climb

A short wall on the start ledge. Hold into it to climb. Space wall-jumps away and keeps extra horizontal speed.

## Driving (Linux)

- Confirm. Walk right into the column. Screenshot `wall.png`.
- Hold `moveRight`: y decreases. `climb.png`.
- Space while holding in: leave left and up. `walljump.png`.
- Dash into the wall, climb a beat, wall jump. x-delta bigger than a standing floor jump. `momentum.png`.
